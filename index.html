      GPS accuracy overlay

      On a ride's summary map, colors the route and dots each recorded point by GPS accuracy instead of speed — green is precise, red is imprecise — and flags the single best and worst points. Gaps between dots show where GPS dropped out.

      How far a point may move to reach a road, and (separately) a path, trail or cycleway. Paths sit close to roads and each other, so a smaller distance keeps a road ride from being pulled onto a parallel path. Too small leaves noisy points unsnapped. Only used when snapping is ticked below.
